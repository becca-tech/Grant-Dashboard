Tier 1: $50,000–$100,000 · Tier 2: $100,001–$200,000 · Total pool: $700,000 · No matching funds required · Projects complete by Dec 31, 2028

Application opened July 21, 2026. Closes September 21, 2026. Award announcement October 30, 2026.

Nonprofits and local government entities. SUNI qualifies directly as a 501(c)(3). Must have active SAM/UEI registration — verify or register at sam.gov before applying.

Carrie Tomlinson, UCF Program Manager · CSFS_UCF@colostate.edu · 405-204-8985

Projects within the City of Denver are limited to private property only due to prior grant cycles already funding Denver public land. Street trees, Chaffee Park plantings, and public ROW corridor work do not qualify. The use case for SUNI shifts to: residential lots along corridors with documented canopy gaps, and private business properties on 38th Ave, 41st/Tejon, or 44th/Zuni.

Frame around residential lot tree planting along corridors where tree canopy is below 20% — a criterion most of North Denver meets. Priority is given to communities with less than 20% canopy cover, areas with safety/hazard tree removal needs, and projects integrating inventories with planting. A corridor-scale private property tree planting program (e.g., 38th Ave residential lots between Federal and Tejon) with a community engagement and education component fits cleanly. Eligible activities: tree planting, strategic pruning, tree removal, urban forest inventory, capacity building, community outreach.

Confirm SUNI has an active SAM/UEI registration (required). Contact Carrie Tomlinson at CSFS_UCF@colostate.edu to confirm that a residential/private property corridor tree planting program in Denver qualifies under the current cycle rules. Deadline is September 21 — 8 weeks from now. This is the largest grant on the board by award size.
